The story

When little Mira discovers something sparkling at the edge of the garden, she isn’t sure she’s brave enough to follow it — until a new friend reminds her that courage can be as simple as taking one small step.

Told with warmth, rhythm, and art full of wonder, this is a story about friendship, trying again, and the magic of believing you can.

About the author

Your Name writes and illustrates stories for curious kids. This book began as a sketch on a rainy afternoon and grew into the tale Mira’s readers know today.

When not at the desk, you’ll find them at the library, in the garden, or reading aloud with a very opinionated cat nearby.
